**SharedCue is a shared list app for the people you live and plan with.** You
write something down once (*coffee*, *pay the electricity bill*, *pack the
charger*) and everyone who shares that list sees it, checks it off, and moves
on. Nobody has to send a "can you grab milk?" text, and nobody has to be the
one who remembers everything.

## The problem it solves

In most homes, one person quietly keeps track of everything: what's running
low, which bill is due, what to pack for Saturday. Everyone else asks *them*.
It works until that person is busy, travelling, or simply tired of being the
household's memory.

The usual fixes don't stick. A group chat buries the list under photos and
plans. A note on your phone is only on *your* phone. And most to-do apps are
built for work projects, not for a family with very different levels of
interest in apps. We wrote more about this in
[The list always lives on one person's phone](/blog/the-list-lives-on-one-phone/).

**SharedCue's promise is simple: stop asking, start noticing.** When the list is
shared and always up to date, whoever is at the store just sees the gap and
fills it.

## How it works, in one minute

SharedCue has three building blocks:

- **Groups are the people.** For example, *Home* with your partner, *Mom & Dad*
  with your parents, or *Goa trip* with friends.
- **Lists live inside a group,** like *Groceries*, *Chores* or *Packing*.
- **Items are the things to do,** with an optional note, a reminder, and a
  repeat.

You invite someone to a group with their mobile number. From then on, when one
of you adds, edits or checks off an item, the other sees it too.

## Who it's for

- **Couples and housemates** who are tired of one person tracking the groceries,
  chores and bills alone.
- **Families spread across two homes,** such as an adult child who keeps one group
  for their own home and another for their parents' home, and switches between
  them in one tap.
- **Parents who want to stay independent,** adding their own needs to a shared
  list instead of waiting to be asked.
- **Friends planning a trip,** so the shared things (the first-aid kit, the power
  adapter) get packed exactly once.
- **Families who don't share a first language.** Each person can use SharedCue in
  their own language, from Hindi and Tamil to Spanish and German.

## What makes it different

- **Calm, not corporate.** No projects, no dashboards, no productivity score.
  Just lists, and a **Today** view of what's due.
- **Easy for everyone in the house.** You sign in with your mobile number and a
  one-time code. There's no password to remember.
- **You always know what changed.** A bell on Home shows what others added or
  checked off since you last looked.
- **Works without a signal.** Keep adding and checking off items offline; your
  changes send when you're back online.
- **Private by design.** Your lists are end-to-end encrypted, and SharedCue has
  no ads and no tracking. Read the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
