Once you know the basics (groups hold lists, lists hold items), SharedCue fits
a lot of everyday situations. Here are five setups that work well, with exactly
how to build each one.

If you haven't set up SharedCue yet, start with the
[features guide](/blog/getting-started-with-sharedcue/).

---

## 1. The grocery list that's always current

**The problem:** someone is at the store, and the list is in someone else's head.

**The setup:**

- **Group:** *Home*, with everyone you live with.
- **List:** *Groceries*.
- **Habit:** the moment something runs low, add it. Use a **note** for the
  details that matter ("2 liters, full cream", "the blue one, not the green").

Whoever shops checks items off as they go. Everyone else sees them disappear,
so nothing gets bought twice.

**Tip:** check the bell on Home before you leave. It shows what others added
since you last looked.

## 2. Chores that don't need a reminder from you

**The problem:** the same chores come round every week, and someone always has
to remind everyone.

**The setup:**

- **Group:** *Home*.
- **List:** *Chores*.
- **Items with repeats:** *Water the plants* repeating **Daily**, *Take out the
  trash* **Weekly**, *Change the water filter* **Monthly**.

When a repeating item is checked off, it comes back for the next time on its
own. Anything due today shows up in the **Today** section on Home.

## 3. Packing for a group trip

**The problem:** everyone packs their own things, nobody is sure who is bringing
the shared things, and you end up with two first-aid kits and no power adapter.

**The setup:**

- **Group:** one per trip, like *Goa trip*, with everyone going.
- **Lists:** *Shared stuff* (tent, speaker, first-aid kit, adapter) and
  *To book* (tickets, stays, cabs).
- **Habit:** put your name in the item's note when you take something on, like
  "Rahul has it".

When the trip's over, the group's creator can delete it from **Manage group**.

## 4. Looking after parents from another city

**The problem:** you look after two homes, yours and your parents', and the
details of theirs arrive in scattered phone calls.

**The setup:**

- **Group:** *Mom & Dad*, with your parents (and any siblings who help).
- **Lists:** *Medicines*, *Groceries*, *Home repairs*.
- **Items with repeats:** medicine refills **Monthly**, a daily *Evening walk*
  or *BP check* **Daily**.

Invite your parents so they can add what *they* need themselves, instead of
waiting to be asked. Each person can use SharedCue in their own language. Keep
this group separate from your own *Home* group and switch between the two on
Home with one tap.

## 5. House projects, big and small

**The problem:** "fix the leaking tap" and "repaint the balcony" live in the same
mental pile as "buy stamps", and none of them get done.

**The setup:**

- **Group:** *Home*.
- **Lists:** *Quick fixes* for the 10-minute jobs, and a separate list per big
  project, like *Balcony repaint*.
- **Habit:** use **notes** for measurements, paint colours and the plumber's
  number, and set a **reminder** for the day you've planned to do it.

Short lists get finished. When a project list is done, rename it or delete it
with the pencil in **Manage group**.

---

## Pick one and start today

You don't need all five. Start with the one that causes the most "did you…?"
texts in your house, set it up in two minutes, and invite one person. SharedCue
works best when it quietly replaces a message you'd otherwise have to send.
